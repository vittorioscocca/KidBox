How it works

From first open to an organized family, in three steps

No long setup: you start, and the family joins you.

On iPhone, Android or in the browser. Two screens and your family is ready: free, no card, no expiry.

2

Invite the others with a link

Partner, grandparents, nanny: send an invite link (valid for 7 days) and whoever opens it joins the family. No limit on members.

Add what matters

Visits, documents, to-dos, calendar: everyone sees them. The AI assistant reads your documents and suggests what to do. You can try Pro free for 14 days.

Questions

What people usually ask

How much does it cost?

You can start free and stay on the Free plan forever. Pro and Max are paid, monthly or yearly, and a single subscription covers the whole family: prices are right above.

How many family members can I invite?

As many as you need: members have no limit and you don't pay per person.

Can I try Pro before paying?

Yes, free for 14 days: you turn it on from the app with one tap, no card. At the end you go back to Free and lose nothing you saved.

Is our data safe?

Documents, messages, notes, passwords and wallet are encrypted with a family key. You share your location only if you want to, and you can turn it off any time. Details are in the privacy policy.

Do I need to download an app?

There is an app for iPhone and Android; on a computer, or in any browser, there is the web app at app.kidboxapp.com, with the same account.

How does the AI work, and how many messages do I get?

The assistant uses your plan's messages: Free includes 5 trial messages, one time only; Pro and Max have a daily and monthly family cap. Meal Plan, Fitness Plan and travel itineraries require Pro or Max.

How do I cancel the subscription?

From the store: subscriptions are managed by the App Store or Google Play, renew automatically and can be cancelled any time.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. How to do it is explained on the Data deletion page.

Which languages is KidBox available in?

Italian, English, Spanish and French, in the app and on the website. The Alexa skill is Italian only.
