• Eventi del calendario: puoi farti avvisare prima, con l'anticipo che scegli tu.

<en-US>
• Calendar and to-dos together: from "+" you choose Event or Reminder, and every to-do with a date shows up in the calendar while staying in its list.
• Urgent reminders: they ring even on silent, like an alarm.
• Calendar events: you can be alerted in advance, with the notice you choose.
• Health: search in treatments, tests and vaccinations, newest first.
• Profile: if you sign in with email and password, you can change it in the app.
